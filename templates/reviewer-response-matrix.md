## Status vocabulary

- **Open** — concern not yet answered.
- **Partial** — answer exists but evidence/change is incomplete.
- **Closed** — direct answer + sufficient evidence + manuscript change align.
- **Not actionable** — positive/descriptive comment, no response needed.
