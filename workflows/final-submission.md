# Final Submission Workflow

## 1. Scientific freeze
Confirm that no decision-critical A/B issue remains and that all major reviewer concerns have a direct answer. Read Results, Discussion, and Conclusion without the response letter to verify that the evidence and interpretation stand on their own.

## 2. Package consistency
Compare clean manuscript, response letter, highlighted manuscript, figures/tables, supplementary video, and supplementary files.

## 3. Terminology freeze
Search for old method names, variables, plot legends, map labels, and stale video overlays.

## 4. PDF preflight
Check page count, paper size, file size, embedded fonts, missing refs/figures, anonymity, figure readability, and clean-vs-diff scientific identity.

## 5. Response-letter preflight
Check comment order, numbering, omitted-positive-comment explanation, wording preservation, response locations, and response+diff limits.

## 6. Multimedia preflight
Check terminology, experiment role labels, playback speed, result tables, old variable names, README/Summary.

## 7. Upload verification
After upload, confirm the portal lists the intended file types and final filenames. When the portal allows downloads, retrieve the submitted files and compare them with the local final versions; use checksums if the portal preserves the original bytes. If downloads are unavailable or the portal transforms the files, record that limitation and verify the portal preview or receipt instead. Never report a download check as completed when it was not possible.

Then stop.
