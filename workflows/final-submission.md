# Final Submission Workflow

## 1. Scientific freeze
Confirm no unresolved A-level issue and no major reviewer concern remains open.

## 2. Package consistency
Compare clean manuscript, response letter, highlighted manuscript, figures/tables, supplementary video, and supplementary files.

## 3. Terminology freeze
Search for old method names, variables, plot legends, map labels, and stale video overlays.

## 4. PDF preflight
Check page count, paper size, file size, embedded fonts, missing refs/figures, anonymity, figure readability, and clean-vs-diff scientific identity.

## 5. Response-letter preflight
Check comment order, numbering, omitted-positive-comment explanation, wording preservation, response locations, and response+diff limits.

## 6. Multimedia preflight
Check terminology, experiment role labels, playback speed, result tables, old variable names, README/Summary.

## 7. Upload verification
After upload, download submitted files from the portal and inspect them again.

Then stop.
